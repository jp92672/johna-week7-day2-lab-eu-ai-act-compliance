Case	Intended category

1	Prohibited	Article 5(1)(f) bans AI systems that infer emotions of a natural person in the workplace, outside narrow medical or safety grounds. 
Coaching, wellbeing scoring and performance review are none of those.	Everything about the framing points at Annex III employment: HR context, human override, no automatic dismissal. A first pass reviewer classifies it as high risk with controls. The override does not save it; Article 5 has no controls escape hatch.	That "stress detection" is acoustic health monitoring and therefore a safety use. Weak: the exception is read narrowly and the output feeds performance review, not safety.

2	High risk	Annex III point 3: AI used to determine access or admission to education and vocational training, and to evaluate learning outcomes. Both models sit squarely in it.	Nothing hidden here, it is meant to be the clean Annex III case. The subtlety is the postcode level completion feature, which is a proxy discrimination and Article 10 data governance problem, not a classification problem.	
That 90% instructor confirmation counts as meaningful human oversight. Weak: rubber stamping is the textbook Article 14 automation bias failure.

3	Limited risk / transparency	Article 50 only. 50(1) for the chatbot, 50(2) machine readable marking of synthetic images and generated text, 50(4) disclosure where generated imagery depicts a real place. No Annex III area is touched.	Buyers and renters being affected by a housing related output baits a jump to Annex III. Housing is not in Annex III; consumer credit is, staging photos are not.	That virtually furnishing a real apartment is a deepfake of an existing scene under 50(4). That one is genuinely arguable, and the stronger risk is national unfair commercial practices law, not the AI Act.

4	Minimal risk	Defect detection and predictive maintenance on machinery. No Annex III area, no Article 50 interaction with a person, no Article 5 practice.	Cameras over workers plus badge scans in the same log reads as workplace monitoring, which would be Annex III point 4(b). It is not, because nothing evaluates or allocates work to individuals.	That incidental worker footage plus badge data makes performance inference trivially available. Fair as a governance point; it is a GDPR Article 6 and Betriebsrat matter under section 87 BetrVG, not an AI Act tier.


Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI) entered into force 27 July 2026. Effect on these four cases:

Case 1: Article 5 prohibitions have applied since 2 February 2025. Live now. Deny.
Case 2: Annex III obligations now apply from 2 December 2027, not 2 August 2026. Approve with controls, sequenced against that date.
Case 3: Article 50 was not deferred and applies from 2 August 2026. This is the only case with an obligation that bit this month.
Case 4: unaffected.
